04 — One more thing

This page only displays information that can be obtained from your public network connection.

The info shown here might not tell the whole story about who you are, but that doesn't mean you're anonymous. You can still be tracked, fingerprinted, or identified through other things your browser gives away. And yeah, I mean it: NO ONE is 100% anonymous on the internet. Your browser might reveal way more than you think. Even with Tor Browser, you're not magically invisible. Stay careful, know what you're exposing, and don't assume you're completely anonymous online.
